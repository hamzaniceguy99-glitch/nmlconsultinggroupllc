How we work

A Florida consultancy, and not your attorney

NML Consulting Group LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, document number L21000392722, filed in 2021, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations.

We do operational and administrative consulting. We are not a law firm, and health care has a category of questions where that distinction really matters: physician self-referral, the Anti-Kickback Statute, corporate practice of medicine, fee-splitting and payer contract terms all need qualified health care counsel before anything is implemented. We are also not a billing company, not a staffing agency and not clinicians — we give no clinical advice. Where we touch records, a business associate agreement is signed first.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Can you tell us if this referral arrangement is allowed?

No. Stark and Anti-Kickback questions are legal questions with serious consequences, and they need health care counsel before anything is implemented. We will tell you when you have reached one.

How long does credentialing take?

Months, and the timeline belongs to the payers rather than to us. Anyone promising a date is guessing. What we can do is start early, track each payer and chase weekly.

Can you do our billing?

No. We are not a billing company and submit no claims. We will work alongside yours, and the operational problems we find often explain the billing ones.

Do we need a compliance programme?

Almost certainly something written, proportionate to your size. A template bought and never opened provides no protection, which is why we build it around how you actually work.

Will this increase our revenue?

We will not promise a figure. Finding where schedule time is lost and getting clinicians enrolled sooner usually helps, but outcomes depend on what actually gets implemented.

Do you need access to patient records?

Only where an engagement genuinely requires it, and a business associate agreement is signed first.
